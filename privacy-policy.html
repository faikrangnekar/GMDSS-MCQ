Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Introduction

GMDSS MCQ ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal and technical information to provide and maintain our services:

Account & Identity Data: Name, display name, email address, and unique user identifier.

Educational & Usage Data: Quiz attempts, response histories, scores, completion status, and log activity.

Transaction & Financial References: Payment confirmation status, transaction IDs, order references, and purchase timestamps. (Note: We do not process or store full credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, bank passwords, or PINs on our servers).

Technical & Log Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and access timestamps collected automatically during platform interactions.

3. Payment Processing

Payment transactions are managed entirely by our third-party payment gateway providers. Financial data provided during checkout is governed by the respective payment processor’s privacy policy and security standards.

4. Legal Basis & How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

Service Delivery: To manage accounts, enable paid access, record study progress, and administer tests.

Transactional Communication: To verify orders, process eligible refunds, and send service updates or support responses.

Security & Misuse Prevention: To detect and prevent security breaches, unauthorized sharing, fraud, or scraping.

Legal Compliance: To meet bookkeeping obligations, respond to lawful legal process, and defend legal claims.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers acting as data processors solely to operate our infrastructure:

Supabase: Database hosting and user authentication management.

GitHub Pages: Static website hosting and infrastructure distribution.

Payment Gateways: Processing secure online payments and transaction confirmations.

These service providers access data strictly to perform designated tasks on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations.

6. International Data Transfers

If you access our platform from outside the country where our servers or service providers are located, your information may be transferred across international borders. By using the platform, you consent to such transfers in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

7. Data Retention and Security

Retention: We retain personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide services, maintain transaction records, enforce agreements, and comply with statutory retention requirements.

Security: We employ administrative, physical, and technical safeguards (such as encrypted database connections via Supabase) designed to protect data. However, no internet transmission method or electronic storage system is 100% secure.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

Correction: Request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

Deletion: Request the erasure of your personal data, subject to legal record retention exceptions.

Objection / Restriction: Request that we restrict or stop processing your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact distresslearning@gmail.com.

9. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will take effect immediately upon posting the updated policy on our website. Continued use of the platform after changes indicates acceptance of the updated terms.
