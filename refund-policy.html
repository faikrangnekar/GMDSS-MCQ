Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Nature of Digital Goods

GMDSS MCQ provides digital access to online GMDSS practice questions, mock tests, and learning materials ("Digital Content"). Due to the immediate digital delivery and non-tangible nature of our Digital Content, all sales are considered final and non-refundable once access to the service has been activated or logged into, except as expressly provided in this Policy or required by mandatory applicable consumer laws.

2. Refund Eligibility Criteria

A refund request will only be evaluated under the following specific conditions:

Duplicate Charges: You were billed more than once for a single transaction due to a technical error during checkout.

Non-Delivery / Technical Activation Failure: Payment was successfully processed and debited, but access was not granted to your account, and our support team is unable to resolve the technical delivery issue within three (3) business days of receiving your notice.

3. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will not be issued under the following conditions:

  • Change of mind after payment has been completed and digital access has been made available.
  • Dissatisfaction with test scores, exam results, or official examination outcomes.
  • Inability to access the platform due to user-side internet connectivity issues, incompatible hardware, or failure to meet system requirements.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated due to a violation of our Terms and Conditions.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund under eligible circumstances:

Email distresslearning@gmail.com using the email address associated with your registered account.

Provide the transaction/order reference ID, date of payment, and a detailed description of the payment or technical issue.

Security Warning: Do NOT include your password, debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account credentials in your email.

5. Resolution & Processing Timeline

We will acknowledge receipt of your request and review the transaction within three (3) business days.

If approved, the refund will be initiated to your original payment method via our payment gateway provider.

Refund processing times vary by bank and payment provider, typically taking 5 to 10 business days to reflect in your account statement.

6. Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy limits, restricts, or excludes any mandatory statutory rights or consumer guarantees guaranteed to you under applicable local laws that cannot be lawfully excluded or modified.
